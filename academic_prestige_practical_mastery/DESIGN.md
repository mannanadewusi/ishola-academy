## Brand & Style

The brand personality projects institutional authority, pragmatic excellence, and steadfast integrity. Tailored for ambitious Nigerian professionals, career switchers, and field practitioners, the visual atmosphere rejects flashy internet-marketing tropes, faux urgency, and exaggerated claims in favor of rigorous, calm, and collegiate confidence. 

The aesthetic is an editorial-modern hybrid: it pairs the dignity and permanence of classic academia with the crisp, efficient ergonomics of high-tier modern enterprise software. The experience feels bespoke, credible, and grounded, evoking the quiet assurance of a world-class polytechnic or professional guild. Generous breathing room, structural precision, subtle hairline borders, and deliberate typographic rhythm inspire trust and sustained focus across every touchpoint.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of grounded scholarship and calm clarity, anchored by deep nautical blues and warmed by unbleached paper tones.

- **Primary (`#0F1B2B`):** Deep Navy. Represents discipline, authority, and permanence. Used for primary interactive triggers, primary headings, top-level navigation shells, and high-impact structural blocks. Tonal step `#162438` is reserved for hovered states, while `#1E3A5F` serves as an active state and secondary brand container.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C59B4B`):** Muted Brass Gold. Signifies achievement, credentialing, and accreditation. Applied sparingly to badges, course verification emblems, rating stars, and subtle border highlights. Tones scale to `#D4AF37` for illuminated states and `#B38634` for accessible text links on light grounds.
- **Surfaces & Canvases (`#FBF9F5`, `#F5F1EA`, `#FFFFFF`):** The default canvas is warm ivory (`#FBF9F5`), preventing optical fatigue. Cards and elevated modules utilize pure `#FFFFFF` to float cleanly against the ivory field, with `#F5F1EA` designated for recessed inputs and data headers.
- **Text & Contrast (`#111827`, `#475569`, `#94A3B8`):** Lead headlines sit in crisp obsidian navy (`#111827`), long-form instructional copy is cast in slate charcoal (`#475569`) to guarantee WCAG AAA contrast, and metadata/captions use `#94A3B8`.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E7E1D6`, `#E2E8F0`):** Fine, warm stone boundaries enforce organization without visual weight.
- **Dedicated Conversion Accent:** WhatsApp integration triggers utilize an authentic deep emerald tint (`#128C7E` / `#075E54`) balanced precisely so as not to disrupt the navy and gold institutional baseline.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects the core ethos of theoretical depth backed by technical execution:

- **Editorial Serif (Merriweather):** Reserved for institutional titles, program headlines, and narrative statements. It lends literary weight, historical credibility, and gravitas without compromising digital legibility.
- **Contemporary Workhorse Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs structural UI, body copy, module descriptions, data readouts, and form fields. Its open counters and geometric balance preserve crisp legibility across varying mobile screens, low-bandwidth connections, and dense curricula listings.
- **Editorial Polish Rules:** Display and headline levels should leverage tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain cohesion. Uppercase styling is permitted strictly on `label-sm` tracking tokens for small module category labels, course tiers, and syllabus index badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptable 12-column grid system for desktop (`1280px` max content constraint), reflowing to an 8-column system on tablets (`768px` to `1024px`), and a single-column 4-field fluid grid on mobile devices (`360px` to `767px`).

- **Vertical Cadence:** Built around an 8px base rhythm. Section padding maintains a minimum of `space-xxl` on desktop to convey academic restraint and calm clarity, dropping to `space-xl` on mobile devices.
- **Mobile-First Data Architecture:** Course syllabus modules, field-survey tools, and tuition breakdowns collapse from multi-column grid matrices into ordered, single-column accordion architectures on small screens.
- **Floating Action Margin:** On mobile viewports, the bottom layout includes a dedicated 80px safe-zone margin to accommodate the fixed direct WhatsApp and enrollment actions without overlapping instructional content.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic neon glows and hyper-saturated 3D drops, utilizing instead layered surfaces, natural light diffusion, and hairline outlines that feel tangible and paper-like:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Base):* Warm Ivory canvas (`#FBF9F5`).
  - *Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):* Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px perimeter outline of stone beige (`#E7E1D6`).
  - *Tier 2 (Elevated & Active Surfaces):* Floating modals, pinned navigation, and course enrollment drawers utilize pure white surfaces backed by an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with Navy: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 27, 43, 0.08)`.
- **Hairline Outlines:** All content cards and input boundaries rely on subtle 1px low-contrast borders (`#E7E1D6`) to ensure distinct shape boundaries under outdoor or high-glare field survey environments without heavy shading.

## Shapes

The interface balances traditional institutional geometry with accessible modern touch surfaces:

- **Corner Radius Standard:** The base roundedness is `0.5rem` (8px), applied across standard content cards, input fields, and action buttons. 
- **Large Cards & Modals:** Utilize `1rem` (16px) for module showcases and syllabus overviews to soften structured analytical tables.
- **Badges & Pills:** Accreditation pills, cohort status tags, and micro-labels utilize a fully rounded pill style (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from interactive rectangular controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Enrollment / Confirmation):** Deep Navy background (`#0F1B2B`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), 0.5rem radius, padded with 0.875rem vertical and 1.75rem horizontal spacing. Smooth hover transition to `#162438`.
- **Secondary Action (Syllabus Preview / Outline):** Transparent background, 1.5px solid Navy border (`#0F1B2B`), navy text. Hover fills to light slate tint (`rgba(15, 27, 43, 0.04)`).
- **Direct Advisory (WhatsApp Inquiry):** Rich dark teal-green (`#128C7E`) with pure white text and integrated icon. Prominently displays the verified desk direct line (`+234 913 021 8969`).

### Cards & Modules
- Structured on pure `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E7E1D6` border and 0.5rem to 1rem corner radius.
- Headers within cards are separated with a hairline divider. Course detail metadata (e.g., duration, software stack, certification level) appears in tabular row formats using `body-sm` in slate charcoal.

### Chips & Badges
- **Accreditation / Track Chips:** Subtle warm gold fill (`#C59B4B` at 12% opacity) paired with `#B38634` text and 1px border (`#C59B4B` at 30% opacity).
- **Status Pills (Live Cohort / Enrolling):** Soft warm sage or navy tint, uppercase `label-sm` tracking.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Pure white or `#F5F1EA` recessed background, bounded by 1px `#E7E1D6`. Focus states shift border to `#0F1B2B` with a 2px outer aura in warm gold (`rgba(197, 155, 75, 0.25)`).

### Verification & Trust Modules
- Dedicated institutional seal blocks featuring crisp linear SVG icons (compass, theodolite, verified badge) in `#C59B4B` paired with high-contrast instructor credentials and license numbers.
