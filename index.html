Ibrahim Jamiu first discovered online survey opportunities while searching for genuine ways to make money online as a student. Like many people, he wanted to find opportunities that could help him earn while managing his other responsibilities.

That curiosity led him to explore how online survey platforms work and understand the processes involved.

Today, he shares what he has learned through Ishola Academy's Survey Training, helping others understand the opportunities, requirements and realities of getting started.

His approach is built around one important message: online surveys are not a get-rich-quick scheme. They require patience, time, consistency and, depending on the platform, investment in certain tools before getting started.

His goal is to help learners approach the opportunity with better understanding and realistic expectations.
